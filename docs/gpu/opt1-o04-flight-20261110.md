## CPU profile while glmark2 build runs (perf -a), both stacks

The kernel GPU worker spends ~18 % of all CPU in `g5p_rv3_fw_touch` plus ~16 % in dcache clean/invalidate. That is
the firmware RAM sampler, reading the shared regions every 1–2 ms. It's a large hot-path CPU cost on a 2-core SoC,
and the next kernel target.

## Fixes verified on device (same day, opt1 kernel, BARE)

- **M3 (o04c, `g5p-04/submit-overhead`)**, cause: main's 8eb57990e07 widened every transfer stage to
  TRANSFER|GEOM|FRAG on G5P too, so Zink's per-batch upload barrier waited on the previous render's
  geometry and fragment work. glmark2 build: tex1 376, o04 + `G9P_NO_GPU_BLIT=1` 372, o04c 377,
  o04c + `PVR_G5P_SIGNAL_SHARE=0` 378 fps. Per submit (tex1 → o04c): TRANSFER 16 → 14, CREATE/DESTROY 9 → 8,
  WAIT 2 → 2, ETIME 0. The full census equals tex1 (build 375, texture 343, shading 332, pulsar 393, desktop 38,
  shadow 89, ideas 278, jellyfish 247).
- **M2/M2b (o04b, `g5p-04/lost-context`):** terrain and refract no longer hang or segfault. No OOM, and the arena stays
  at 9/6 chunks after 10 s and 30 s. glmark2 build 307 (no M3), Aquarium 500 31 fps without a crash.
  The SuperTuxKart `--benchmark` (black_forest replay) completes, rc 0 in 59 s, but with 16,438 G5P refusals and
  a lost context, so its fps (steady 11, typical 18) aren't valid yet. It needs: vertex attribute format 75,
  vertex inputs at locations 31/32, `load_frag_coord .z`, and discard in destination-reading MRT programs.
- **Combined ship head:** `g5p-04/release` 3a00235254b (both fixes), ICD + GL builds PASS.
